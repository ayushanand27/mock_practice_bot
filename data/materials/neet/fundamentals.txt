================================================================================
PHYSICS — KINEMATICS & PROJECTILES (NEET)
================================================================================

Scalars vs vectors
- Distance (scalar) vs displacement (vector). Speed vs velocity.
- Average velocity = total displacement / total time.
- Instantaneous velocity is dx/dt; acceleration a = dv/dt.

Equations of motion (constant acceleration a)
- v = u + a t
- s = u t + (1/2) a t^2
- v^2 = u^2 + 2 a s
- Always pick a positive direction; g ≈ 9.8 m/s^2 downward near Earth.

Projectile motion (level ground, launch speed u at angle θ)
- ux = u cos θ (constant); uy = u sin θ initially.
- Time of flight T = (2 u sin θ) / g
- Maximum height H = (u^2 sin^2 θ) / (2 g)
- Range R = (u^2 sin 2θ) / g ; maximum range at θ = 45° (same level).

================================================================================
PHYSICS — NEWTON & ENERGY
================================================================================

Newton's laws
- 1st: inertia — constant velocity unless net force.
- 2nd: F_net = m a
- 3rd: action-reaction pairs act on different bodies.

Energy
- Kinetic K = (1/2) m v^2
- Gravitational PE near Earth ≈ m g h
- Work-energy: net work = change in kinetic energy.

================================================================================
CHEMISTRY — ATOMIC STRUCTURE & PERIODICITY
================================================================================

Particles
- Electron e−, proton p+, neutron n0. Atomic number Z = protons.
- Mass number A = protons + neutrons. Isotopes: same Z, different A (e.g. C-12, C-14).

Electron configuration
- Aufbau, Pauli, Hund rules.
- Example: N (Z=7) → 1s2 2s2 2p3 (three unpaired p electrons).

Periodic trends
- Atomic radius generally decreases across a period.
- Ionization energy generally increases across a period; decreases down a group.
- Electronegativity rises toward F.

================================================================================
CHEMISTRY — MOLES & STOICHIOMETRY
================================================================================

- 1 mole = 6.022 × 10^23 entities (Avogadro number N_A).
- n = m / M (moles = mass / molar mass).
- Molarity M = moles of solute / litres of solution.
- Limiting reagent: reactant that finishes first and caps product amount.

================================================================================
BIOLOGY (NEET) — CELL & GENETICS
================================================================================

Cell basics
- Plasma membrane: phospholipid bilayer, selective permeability, fluid mosaic model.
- Mitochondria: site of cellular respiration / ATP (powerhouse).
- Chloroplast (plants): photosynthesis.
- Nucleus: DNA; nucleolus makes rRNA. Ribosomes: protein synthesis.
- Endoplasmic reticulum / Golgi: processing and packaging of proteins/lipids.

Cell division
- Mitosis: identical diploid daughters (growth, repair).
- Meiosis: haploid gametes; crossing over increases variation.
- Stages of mitosis: prophase, metaphase, anaphase, telophase (+ cytokinesis).

Genetics
- Mendel: segregation and independent assortment (with caveats for linked genes).
- Genotype vs phenotype. Dominant vs recessive alleles.
- DNA: double helix, A-T and G-C base pairing; replication is semi-conservative.

Exam tip: NEET weights Biology heavily — master NCERT Biology line-by-line, then Physics/Chemistry numericals.

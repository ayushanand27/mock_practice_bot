INDIAN POLITY (Prelims essentials)
================================================================================

Constitution basics
- Adopted 26 Nov 1949; came into force 26 Jan 1950 (Republic Day).
- Preamble: Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic, Republic; Justice, Liberty, Equality, Fraternity.
- "Socialist" and "Secular" added by 42nd Amendment (1976); "Unity of the Nation" → "Unity and Integrity" also 42nd.

Fundamental Rights (Part III)
- Arts. 12–35. Enforceable against the State (Art. 12).
- Right to Equality (14–18), Freedom (19–22), against Exploitation (23–24), Religion (25–28), Cultural & Educational (29–30), Constitutional Remedies (32 — "heart and soul", per Ambedkar).
- Art. 19: six freedoms (speech, assembly, association, movement, residence, profession) — subject to reasonable restrictions.
- Art. 21: life and personal liberty; expanded by judiciary (privacy, livelihood, clean environment, etc.).
- Art. 32: writs — habeas corpus, mandamus, prohibition, certiorari, quo warranto. High Courts: Art. 226 (wider).

DPSP & Fundamental Duties
- DPSPs (Part IV, Arts. 36–51): non-justiciable guidelines; aim for welfare state. Inspired partly by Irish Constitution.
- Fundamental Duties (Art. 51A): added by 42nd Amendment on Swaran Singh Committee recommendations; originally 10, 11th via 86th (education of child 6–14).

Parliament & Executive
- Lok Sabha: max 552 (practical strength lower after delimitation freeze nuances — revise current figures from latest sources). Rajya Sabha: permanent house; 1/3 retire every 2 years.
- Money Bill: only LS; Speaker’s certificate final (Art. 110). RS can recommend within 14 days; LS may accept/reject.
- President: executive head; elected by Electoral College (elected MPs + elected MLAs). Impeachment for violation of Constitution (Art. 61).
- PM: head of Council of Ministers; collective responsibility to Lok Sabha (Art. 75).
- Ordinance (Art. 123): when Parliament not in session; must be laid before Houses; ceases if not approved in 6 weeks of reassembly.

Judiciary & federalism
- Supreme Court: original, appellate, advisory jurisdiction. Judicial review of legislation.
- Basic Structure doctrine (Kesavananda Bharati, 1973): Parliament cannot amend Constitution to destroy basic features.
- Centre–State: Seventh Schedule — Union, State, Concurrent Lists. Residuary with Centre (Art. 248).
- Emergency: National (352), State/President’s Rule (356), Financial (360).

Local bodies & elections
- 73rd / 74th Amendments: Panchayats / Municipalities; State Election Commission; Finance Commission for local bodies.
- Election Commission of India (Art. 324): superintendence of elections to Parliament, State legislatures, President, VP.
- Anti-defection (10th Schedule): disqualification for voluntary give-up of membership / voting against party whip (with exceptions).

MODERN INDIAN HISTORY (Prelims flavour)
================================================================================

Company to Crown
- Battle of Plassey (1757), Buxar (1764) → dual system / Diwani of Bengal.
- Regulating Act 1773, Pitt’s India Act 1784, Charter Acts; 1858 Government of India Act after 1857 Revolt — Crown rule, Secretary of State.

Socio-religious reform
- Brahmo Samaj (Raja Ram Mohan Roy), Arya Samaj (Dayanand), Aligarh Movement (Sir Syed), Theosophical Society, Satyashodhak (Phule), Narayana Guru — social reform linked to nationalism.

National movement milestones
- INC founded 1885 (A.O. Hume; first session W.C. Bonnerjee, Bombay).
- Moderates vs Extremists; Surat Split 1907; Lucknow Pact 1916 (Congress–League).
- Partition of Bengal 1905 → Swadeshi; annulled 1911.
- Home Rule (Tilak, Annie Besant); Montagu–Chelmsford / GoI Act 1919; Rowlatt, Jallianwala Bagh 1919.
- Non-Cooperation (1920–22), Civil Disobedience / Dandi March 1930, Round Table Conferences, Poona Pact 1932 (Ambedkar–Gandhi on depressed classes).
- GoI Act 1935: provincial autonomy, federal scheme (Centre never fully implemented as designed).
- Quit India 1942; INA trials; Cabinet Mission 1946; Mountbatten Plan; Independence 15 Aug 1947.

Constitution-making
- Constituent Assembly first met Dec 1946; Drafting Committee chaired by B.R. Ambedkar; Constitution adopted 26 Nov 1949.

INDIAN ECONOMY (Prelims basics)
================================================================================

National income concepts
- GDP: value of final goods/services produced within domestic territory in a year.
- GNP = GDP + net factor income from abroad.
- NDP / NNP: subtract depreciation. Factor cost vs market price: taxes − subsidies.
- Real vs nominal: real uses constant prices (inflation-adjusted).

Inflation & money
- CPI / WPI: price indices; RBI’s inflation targeting framework uses CPI (flexible inflation targeting with government).
- Demand-pull vs cost-push inflation. Stagflation: slow growth + high inflation.
- Repo / reverse repo / CRR / SLR: tools affecting liquidity and credit.

Fiscal concepts
- Budget: receipts (revenue + capital) and expenditure (revenue + capital).
- Fiscal deficit: total expenditure − (revenue receipts + non-debt capital receipts).
- Revenue deficit: revenue expenditure − revenue receipts. Primary deficit ≈ fiscal deficit − interest payments.
- GST: destination-based indirect tax; CGST/SGST/IGST; GST Council.

Planning & reforms
- Planning Commission → NITI Aayog (2015): think tank, cooperative federalism (not a planning body allocating Plan funds like before).
- 1991 reforms: LPG — liberalisation, privatisation, globalisation; balance-of-payments crisis context.
- Priority sector lending, financial inclusion (Jan Dhan), DBT — revise scheme names/years from current affairs capsules.

External sector
- CAD: current account deficit. Forex reserves; BoP identity.
- FDI vs FPI; WTO basics (MFN, national treatment) at definition level for prelims.

================================================================================

Physical / India
- Monsoon: seasonal reversal of winds; southwest monsoon main rainfall; El Niño association with weaker monsoon (probabilistic, not absolute).
- Himalayas / Peninsular plateau / Indo-Gangetic plain; major drainage: Himalayan vs Peninsular rivers.
- Soils: alluvial, black (regur), red, laterite — crop associations (e.g. black soil ↔ cotton).

Environment & ecology
- Biodiversity hotspots in India (Western Ghats, Himalayas, Indo-Burma, Sundaland — revise exact list).
- Protected areas: National Parks, Wildlife Sanctuaries, Biosphere Reserves; Project Tiger.
- Climate change: UNFCCC, Kyoto, Paris Agreement — NDCs; India’s renewable energy push (revise latest targets from current sources).

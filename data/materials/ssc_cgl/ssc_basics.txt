================================================================================
QUANTITATIVE APTITUDE
================================================================================

Percentages
- x% of y = (x/100) * y.
- If A increases by r%, multiply by (1 + r/100). Decrease by r%: (1 − r/100).
- Successive changes: multiply factors. Example: +20% then −20% → ×1.2×0.8 = 0.96 (net −4%).

Ratio and proportion
- a:b = m:n means a = m k, b = n k for some k.
- If a/b = c/d then a d = b c (cross multiply).
- Partnership: profit share ∝ (capital × time).

Simple and compound interest
- SI = (P × R × T) / 100
- Amount under SI = P + SI
- CI annual: A = P (1 + R/100)^T ; CI = A − P
- For 2 years CI vs SI difference involves interest on first year's interest.

Averages and mixtures
- Average = sum / count.
- Combined average of groups: weighted by sizes.
- Alligation: rule of mixtures for two concentrations.

Time and work
- If A finishes in a days, rate = 1/a job per day.
- Together: rates add (if independent).
- If A is twice as efficient as B, rates 2:1.

Time, speed, distance
- Speed = distance / time. Units: convert km/h ↔ m/s using ×5/18 or ×18/5.
- Average speed for equal distances at speeds a and b: 2ab/(a+b) (harmonic mean).
- Relative speed: same direction |v1−v2|; opposite v1+v2.

Algebra / mensuration (Tier-I flavour)
- (a+b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2 ; (a−b)^2 ; a^2 − b^2 = (a−b)(a+b).
- Area circle π r^2 ; circumference 2 π r.
- Triangle area (1/2) base × height; Pythagoras for right triangles.

Numerical drill examples
- SI on 5000 at 10% for 2 years = 1000; amount = 6000.
- 60 km/h = 60 × 5/18 = 50/3 m/s ≈ 16.67 m/s.

================================================================================
GENERAL INTELLIGENCE & REASONING
================================================================================

Series
- Number series: +/− constant, ×/÷, alternating patterns, squares/cubes.
- Letter series: positional shifts in alphabet (A=1 … Z=26).

Coding-decoding
- Shift cipher (each letter +k), reverse order, or symbol mapping.
- Always test the rule on the given example before applying to the question.

Blood relations
- Draw a small family tree; mark generations clearly.
- Maternal/paternal uncle/aunt, cousin definitions matter.

Syllogisms
- Use Venn diagrams. "All A are B", "Some A are B", "No A is B".
- Conclusions must follow in all possible diagrams (possibility vs certainty).

Directions & seating
- Track facing and left/right carefully; redraw after each turn.
- Circular seating: usually facing centre unless stated; left/right from person's view.

================================================================================
ENGLISH COMPREHENSION
================================================================================

Error spotting
- Subject-verb agreement; articles (a/an/the); prepositions; tense consistency.
- Pronoun reference and parallel structure.

Vocab
- Synonyms / antonyms: high-frequency SSC lists + roots/prefixes.
- One-word substitution and idioms: learn in small daily batches.

Cloze & RC
- Cloze: grammar + context decide the blank.
- Reading comprehension: answer only from the passage; avoid outside knowledge.
- Tone questions: objective, critical, optimistic, etc.

================================================================================
GENERAL AWARENESS
================================================================================

Polity
- Constitution: Preamble, Fundamental Rights, DPSPs, Fundamental Duties.
- Parliament: Lok Sabha, Rajya Sabha; President, PM, Council of Ministers.
- Supreme Court / High Courts — basic jurisdiction ideas.

Geography
- Major rivers (Ganga, Yamuna, Godavari, Krishna, Narmada, Brahmaputra).
- Himalayas / Western Ghats; monsoon basics; soil types intro.

Economy
- GDP, inflation, fiscal deficit (definitions).
- RBI roles; budget highlights (revise current year from reliable summaries).

Science & static GK
- Everyday physics/chemistry/biology one-liners (SI units, vitamins, diseases).
- Awards, books/authors, important days — revise from a trusted static GK capsule.

Current affairs
- Last 6–12 months: national schemes, sports, appointments, international summits.
- Prefer monthly compilations over random scrolling.

Exam tip (Tier-I): accuracy beats volume under negative marking; skip traps and return if time permits.

================================================================================
PHYSICS — KINEMATICS & PROJECTILES
================================================================================

Scalars vs vectors
- Distance (scalar) vs displacement (vector). Speed vs velocity.
- Average velocity = total displacement / total time.
- Instantaneous velocity is dx/dt; acceleration a = dv/dt.

Equations of motion (constant acceleration a)
- v = u + a t
- s = u t + (1/2) a t^2
- v^2 = u^2 + 2 a s
- Always pick a positive direction; g ≈ 9.8 m/s^2 downward near Earth.

Projectile motion (level ground, launch speed u at angle θ)
- ux = u cos θ (constant); uy = u sin θ initially.
- Time of flight T = (2 u sin θ) / g
- Maximum height H = (u^2 sin^2 θ) / (2 g)
- Range R = (u^2 sin 2θ) / g ; maximum range at θ = 45° (same level).
- Horizontal motion is uniform; vertical motion is accelerated by g.

Worked idea
- If u = 20 m/s, θ = 30°, g = 10 m/s^2: T = 2 s, H = 5 m, R = 20√3 ≈ 34.6 m.

================================================================================
PHYSICS — NEWTON & ENERGY
================================================================================

Newton's laws
- 1st: inertia — constant velocity unless net force.
- 2nd: F_net = m a
- 3rd: action-reaction pairs act on different bodies.

Energy
- Kinetic K = (1/2) m v^2
- Gravitational PE near Earth ≈ m g h (choose reference carefully)
- Work-energy: net work = change in kinetic energy.
- Conservative forces: path-independent work; mechanical energy conserved if only they do work.

================================================================================
CHEMISTRY — ATOMIC STRUCTURE & PERIODICITY
================================================================================

Particles
- Electron e−, proton p+, neutron n0. Atomic number Z = protons.
- Mass number A = protons + neutrons. Isotopes: same Z, different A (e.g. C-12, C-14).

Bohr model (hydrogen-like)
- Electron orbits with quantized energy; absorption/emission when jumping levels.
- Limitations: fails for multi-electron atoms and fine structure — quantum model needed.

Electron configuration
- Aufbau: fill lower energy orbitals first.
- Pauli: max 2 electrons per orbital, opposite spins.
- Hund: maximize unpaired electrons in degenerate orbitals before pairing.
- Example: N (Z=7) → 1s2 2s2 2p3 (three unpaired p electrons).

Periodic trends
- Atomic radius generally decreases across a period (effective nuclear charge ↑).
- Ionization energy generally increases across a period; decreases down a group.
- Electronegativity rises toward F.

================================================================================
CHEMISTRY — MOLES & STOICHIOMETRY
================================================================================

- 1 mole = 6.022 × 10^23 entities (Avogadro number N_A).
- n = m / M (moles = mass / molar mass).
- Molarity M = moles of solute / litres of solution.
- Limiting reagent: reactant that finishes first and caps product amount.
- Percent yield = (actual / theoretical) × 100.

Example
- 18 g water is 1 mol H2O; contains 2 mol H atoms and 1 mol O atoms.

================================================================================
MATH (JEE) — LIMITS, DERIVATIVES, INTEGRALS
================================================================================

Limits
- lim (x→0) (sin x)/x = 1 (x in radians).
- lim (x→0) (1 − cos x)/x^2 = 1/2.
- Factor, rationalize, or use standard limits before L'Hôpital when allowed.

Derivatives
- d/dx (x^n) = n x^(n−1)
- Product: (uv)' = u'v + uv'
- Quotient: (u/v)' = (u'v − uv') / v^2
- Chain: d/dx f(g(x)) = f'(g(x)) · g'(x)

Integrals (brief)
- ∫ x^n dx = x^(n+1)/(n+1) + C (n ≠ −1)
- ∫ sin x dx = −cos x + C ; ∫ cos x dx = sin x + C
- Definite integral = area under curve (signed); FTC links derivatives and integrals.

Exam tip: write Given → Formula → Substitute → Units/box the answer for numericals.

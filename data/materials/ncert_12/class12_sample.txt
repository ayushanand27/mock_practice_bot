================================================================================
CLASS 12 PHYSICS — ELECTROSTATICS & CURRENT
================================================================================

Coulomb's law
- F = k |q1 q2| / r^2 ; k = 1/(4 π ε0) ≈ 9 × 10^9 N m^2/C^2.
- Electric field E = F/q (force on a unit positive test charge).
- Potential V: work per unit charge by external agent (slowly) without KE change; E related to −dV/dx in 1D.

Capacitance
- C = Q/V. Parallel plate C = ε0 A / d (vacuum).
- Capacitors store energy U = (1/2) C V^2 = Q^2/(2C).
- Series: 1/C = Σ 1/Ci ; Parallel: C = Σ Ci.

Current electricity
- Ohm's law: V = I R (ohmic). Resistance R = ρ L / A.
- Kirchhoff: junction current sum 0; loop voltage sum 0.
- Series resistors add; parallel: 1/R = Σ 1/Ri.

================================================================================
CLASS 12 CHEMISTRY — SOLUTIONS & ELECTROCHEMISTRY (INTRO)
================================================================================

Solutions
- Molarity, molality, mole fraction, ppm.
- Raoult's law: for ideal solutions, p_i = x_i p_i° (vapour pressure lowering).
- Colligative properties depend on number of solute particles: relative lowering of vapour pressure, boiling point elevation, freezing point depression, osmotic pressure.
- van't Hoff factor i accounts for dissociation/association.

Electrochemistry (high level)
- Galvanic cell: spontaneous redox → electrical energy.
- Electrolytic cell: electrical energy drives non-spontaneous reaction.
- Nernst equation adjusts cell potential for non-standard concentrations.

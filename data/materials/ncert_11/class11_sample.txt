================================================================================
CLASS 11 PHYSICS — UNITS, MEASUREMENTS, MOTION
================================================================================

SI base units
- metre (m), kilogram (kg), second (s), ampere (A), kelvin (K), mole (mol), candela (cd).
- Derived examples: newton N = kg m/s^2 ; joule J = N m ; watt W = J/s.

Dimensional analysis
- Check equation consistency: both sides same dimensions.
- Example: v^2 = u^2 + 2 a s → [L^2 T^-2] on each term.
- Cannot find dimensionless constants (like 1/2 in s = ut + 1/2 at^2) from dimensions alone.

Significant figures
- Reflect measurement precision; multiplication/division → least number of sig figs.
- Addition/subtraction → least decimal places.

Motion in a straight line (links to kinematics)
- Position, displacement, velocity, acceleration graphs.
- Area under v-t graph = displacement; slope of v-t = acceleration.

================================================================================
CLASS 11 CHEMISTRY — BASIC CONCEPTS & STRUCTURE
================================================================================

Mole concept
- 1 mole = 6.022 × 10^23 entities.
- Molar mass links grams ↔ moles. Molar volume of ideal gas at STP ≈ 22.4 L/mol (older STP convention used in many NCERT problems).

Empirical vs molecular formula
- Empirical: simplest whole-number ratio of atoms.
- Molecular: actual count; molecular mass / empirical mass = integer multiplier.

Stoichiometry
- Balanced equation → mole ratios.
- Limiting reagent decides maximum product.
- Concentration: molarity (mol/L), molality (mol/kg solvent) — molality independent of temperature.

Atomic structure (board focus)
- Quantum numbers: n, l, m_l, m_s.
- Orbitals: s, p, d, f shapes/orientations at intro level.
- Electronic configuration using Aufbau, Hund, Pauli.

================================================================================
CLASS 11 BIOLOGY — DIVERSITY & CELL
================================================================================

The Living World
- Taxonomy: classification + nomenclature.
- Binomial nomenclature: Genus species (e.g. Homo sapiens) — Genus capitalised, species lowercase, italicised in print.
- Hierarchy: Kingdom → Phylum/Division → Class → Order → Family → Genus → Species.

Cell: The Unit of Life
- Prokaryotic vs eukaryotic: nucleus, membrane-bound organelles.
- Plant cell: cell wall (cellulose), chloroplasts, large vacuole; animal cell: centrioles often present.
- Plasma membrane: fluid mosaic; selective transport (diffusion, osmosis, active transport).

Biomolecules (intro)
- Carbohydrates, proteins, lipids, nucleic acids — monomers and roles.
- Enzymes: biocatalysts; affected by temperature and pH.
